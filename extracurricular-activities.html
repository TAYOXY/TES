Extra-curricular Activities

A range of creative, practical, performing, and sporting activities gives pupils more ways to explore their interests and develop new skills.

Learning beyond the classroom

Find something to enjoy

From creative expression and practical skills to team sports and classic games, explore the activities available at Treasureland.

Music

Discover rhythm, melody, singing, and musical expression while building confidence and listening skills.

Art & Creativity

Experiment with colour, drawing, painting, and making original creations.

Tie & Dye

Explore patterns, colour combinations, and fabric design through tie-dye craft.

Makeup Artistry

Try age-appropriate creative looks, colour design, and introductory makeup artistry.

Culinary Arts

Get hands-on with pastries, cooking, and baking while learning useful kitchen skills.

Dance

Move to music, learn dance steps, and express ideas through movement and performance.

Sports & Games

Enjoy active sports such as basketball, football, volleyball, and table tennis, alongside ludo, ayo, chess, PlayStation, and Xbox 360 games.

Seamstress & Sewing

Learn about fabric, stitches, simple sewing, and the basics of garment making.
