Our Programs

Discover our comprehensive educational programs designed for every developmental stage

Educational Excellence at Every Stage

At Treasureland Elementary School, we offer carefully crafted programs that cater to the unique developmental needs of children at different ages, ensuring a seamless educational journey from early years to elementary.

Crèche/Toddler Group

Our Crèche and Toddler program provides a nurturing and stimulating environment for our youngest learners. Through play-based activities and gentle guidance, we help toddlers develop essential motor skills, social awareness, and early language abilities in a safe, caring setting.

Sensory development activities
Social and emotional growth
Language and communication skills

Pre-School (18 months to 5 years)

Our Pre-School program follows the Montessori approach, fostering independence, curiosity, and a love for learning. Children engage with specially designed materials that develop cognitive skills, creativity, and social competence through hands-on exploration and guided discovery.

Montessori-based curriculum
Practical life skills development
Sensorial and language activities

Elementary School (5 to 11 years)

Our Elementary School program builds strong academic foundations while nurturing critical thinking, creativity, and character development. We offer a comprehensive curriculum that prepares students for future academic success and lifelong learning through engaging, challenging, and relevant educational experiences.

Core subjects and enrichment programs
STEM and arts integration
Leadership and character development
