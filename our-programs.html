Our Programs

Discover our comprehensive educational programs designed for every developmental stage

Educational Excellence at Every Stage

At Treasureland Elementary School, we offer carefully crafted programs that cater to the unique developmental needs of children at different ages, ensuring a seamless educational journey from early years to elementary.

Montessori Academic

Our Montessori Academic program creates a calm, structured, and engaging learning environment where children build independence, confidence, and a strong foundation in literacy, numeracy, and practical life skills.

Independent learning and self-discipline
Hands-on learning with Montessori materials
Early academic confidence and curiosity

Montessori Foundation

Learning through exploration

Safe beginnings

Nurturing with care and consistency

Creche

Our Creche offers a secure, warm, and affectionate setting for babies and toddlers, helping them feel safe while supporting early sensory, emotional, and motor development in a nurturing environment.

Warm, caring daily routines
Early social and sensory stimulation
Safe, child-centred care

Preschool (Preparatory & Playgroup)

Our Preschool programme blends play, discovery, and guided learning to help children develop early literacy, numeracy, communication, independence, and confidence in preparation for primary school.

Play-based and discovery learning
Language, numeracy and social readiness
Confidence-building routines and activities

Preparatory & Playgroup

Strong early learning foundation

Primary School

Our Primary School programme develops strong foundations in literacy, numeracy, science, creativity, and character, helping pupils become confident thinkers, communicators, and responsible young leaders.

Core subject mastery and enrichment
Critical thinking and creativity
Leadership skills and values

Primary Learning

Building confident future-ready learners
