Our Roots

Treasureland Elementary school as a Montessori school was established on the 19th September 2011 under the proprietorship of Mrs Ebun Elizabeth Ogunyemi and Mr Rapheal Adebola Ogunyemi.

Child-Centered Approach

We embrace a child-centered approach, ensuring that each student's unique needs and potential are at the forefront of our educational practices.

Innovation in Learning

Our teaching and learning policy prioritizes engaging, relevant, and challenging lessons designed to prepare students for life beyond the classroom.

Personalized Support

We believe that excellent teaching forms the foundation for personalized support, enabling every student to achieve their best.
