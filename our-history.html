Our History

The journey of Treasureland Elementary School from inception to excellence

Our Beginning

Treasureland Elementary School (TES) as a Montessori school was established on the 19th September 2011 under the proprietorship of Mrs Ebun Elizabeth Ogunyemi and Mr Rapheal Adebola Ogunyemi.

The couple had secured a two and half acres of land at Government Reservation Area (GRA), Ijebu Ode which serves as the permanent site of the school. When the school started it had seven pupils on roll.

Treasureland has grown tremendously in size and standard with a growing population of over 300 pupils and an excellent work force. Treasureland elementary school has been family supporting from the start. The management is effectively guided by a formidable Board of Governors.

Our Journey of Growth

2011 - The Foundation

School established with 7 founding pupils on September 19th, 2011

2011-2015 - Early Growth

Establishment of permanent site at GRA, Ijebu Ode with 2.5 acres of land

2015-2020 - Expansion

Growth to over 300 pupils and development of excellent workforce

2020-Present - Excellence

Recognition as a leading Montessori institution with strong governance

Our Philosophy

Vision

To promote life-long learning in a natural and caring environment that brings out the inborn talent for growth and self-reliance for a child to face world's challenges.

Core Belief

If you are planning for life, educate children because they are the future leaders.

Foundation

Good foundation that ensures discipline, God fearing and caring children.

Development

Develop high intellectual, mental and physically fit children.

Atmosphere of humility and respect for fellow children

Natural and beautiful environment that helps to inculcate aesthetics value and culture maintenance

Freedom to explore and discover themselves at their pace
